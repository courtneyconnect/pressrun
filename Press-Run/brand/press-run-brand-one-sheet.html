01 · Logo

The camera-head walker on the red crosswalk

App icon (left): always on pure black, red glow kept. Website (right): the same character drawn in parts so it can run across the tiles.

Keep clear space of one tile height around it. Apple Messages for Business needs a version with 10% padding.

05 · Voice

Like a good booking agent texting

Booked ✅ Courtney from Music for a Minute · Fri Nov 13, 2:30pm · Washington Square Park
Sent 🙌 I'll pass their replies here.
  • Short, plain, friendly. One idea per text.
  • Emoji to mark a moment (✅ 🙌 🎬 🔥), not to decorate.
  • Always credit the creator: "Courtney from Music for a Minute".

07 · Do / Don't

Keep it recognizable

  • Do keep backgrounds black or charcoal.
  • Do keep the character slate gray with the red-and-white eye.
  • Do keep everything in one iMessage thread.
  • Don't recolor or outline the red.
  • Don't put the logo on light or busy photos.
  • Don't show phone numbers. They stay hidden until SHARE.
